LABS · SINE-WAVE SPEECH

Speech made of three whistles.

A recorded voice is reduced to three sine tones that follow its formants. Heard first, it sounds like birdsong or whistling. After you hear the original once, the same sine rendering becomes words. Pick a voice and a phrase, then press Analyse + play.

Reads the authored bar/step/nominal-beat provenance from the same score events sent to the AudioWorklet. It counts harmony-boundary gate overlap, long non-chord tones and simultaneous minor-second/tritone onsets. These are descriptive facts, not a musical-quality score.

Pretrained pitch critic
Optional, lazy-loaded browser analysis using Spotify Basic Pitch. It renders a short C4 probe directly through one hash-matched native-build LeanDSP kernel at 22.05 kHz, then compares the model’s inferred note with the intended MIDI pitch. Use Tuning audit for the faster C3/C4/A4 sweep. Best interpreted on pitched instruments; noisy/percussive patterns can legitimately confuse a transcription model.
