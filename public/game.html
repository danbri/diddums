LABS · ADAPTIVE MUSIC DEMO

Music that follows the game.

One endless piece, written a couple of bars ahead from the game's state. Sneaking, exploring and fighting change which parts play, the drum pattern, the energy and the chords, from the next bar line. Low health brings in a heartbeat straight away. Everything is synthesised live by the same LeanDSP instruments as the rest of Diddums.

Drag in the field to move. Enemies close in when they notice you; in Fight mode they come straight for you and cost health on contact.

Press Start. Changes land on the next bar line.

 




How a game drives it

The page uses adaptive.js. A game calls music.set({mode, enemies, health}) whenever its state changes; a plan function maps that state to an arrangement. Nothing else is needed.


const music = await startAdaptive({composition, plan});
music.set({mode: 'fight', enemies: 7});   // re-arranged from the next bar
music.control('heart', 'level', 0.9);      // mix changes act at once


The plan used here: Sneak is drumless with a low drone and sparse plucks; Explore adds chords, a bell melody and a broken beat as enemies appear; Fight switches to an acid bass, brass stabs, arpeggio, saw lead and an industrial kit, on a harder chord progression. More enemies mean more energy (louder, busier parts).
