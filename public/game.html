LABS · ADAPTIVE MUSIC DEMO

Music that follows the game.

Collect the cyan orbs without being caught. Patrols notice you inside their circle and give chase; the gold orb lets you knock them out for seven seconds. Clearing a level changes key and adds a patrol. The score is one endless piece written a couple of bars ahead from how much danger you are in, your health and your power, and every pickup, alarm, hit and knockout answers within a sixteenth note, in key.

Move with arrow keys or WASD, or hold a finger or the mouse where you want to go. Autopilot plays by itself so you can just listen.

Threat 0% · Health 100%
Press Start. Arrangement changes land on the next bar line.

 




What drives what

Threat (patrols chasing you, and how close the nearest is) brings parts in one by one: drone and plucks when unseen; sub bass and a sparse kit when watched; arpeggio, acid bass, saw lead and harder drums as the hunt builds. Health changes the music itself: below half the bell melody and saw lead drop out; near death the chords turn to a flat-second dread progression with a halftime beat, the pad gives way to a drone, and a heartbeat grows louder. Power switches to a heroic progression and house beat. Level changes key.


Events use music.hit(): notes are chosen from the chord playing at that moment and placed on the next 16th note (0.15 s at 100 bpm). Pickups climb the chord as an arpeggio when collected quickly; being spotted sounds a sonar ping on the beat; a hit is a low timpani; a knockout is a beacon blip; levels and game over get short phrases.


const music = await startAdaptive({composition, plan});
music.set({threat: 0.7, health: 0.3});               // re-arranged from the next bar
music.hit('chime', {tones: [0, 1, 2], spread: 1});   // root, third, fifth on the next 16ths
