LABS · ADAPTIVE MUSIC DEMO

Music that follows the game.

Gather the 🍄 mushrooms, then grab the 💰 treasure that appears to reach the next level. 💎 gems are worth more. Big triangles hunt you once you enter their circle; hide among 🌲 trees or in the house, where they cannot follow and you heal. A ⭐ star lets you knock them out for seven seconds. Your companion is the small triangle. The music is one endless piece shaped by your danger, health and power, and every level brings a different musical idiom.

Move with arrow keys or WASD, or hold a finger or the mouse where you want to go. Autopilot plays by itself so you can just listen.

Press Start. Arrangement changes land on the next bar line.

 




What drives what

Threat (hunters chasing you, and how close the nearest is) brings parts in one at a time: drone and a process layer when unseen; sub bass and a sparse kit when watched; arpeggio, acid bass, saw lead and harder drums as the hunt builds. Health changes the music itself: below half the bell melody and saw lead drop out; near death the chords turn to a dread progression with a halftime beat, the pad gives way to a drone, and a heartbeat grows louder. Power switches to a heroic progression and house beat. In the house the music settles.


Each level is a different idiom, not just a new key: night synth (A minor), hijaz (D, the scale of much Arabic and Balkan music, with a koto isorhythm and clave), pentatonic phasing (E, a marimba slipping against the beat, bembé bell), octatonic (F, the half-whole scale of chase music, Amen-style breaks), whole-tone (C, a scattered bell cloud) and pelog (G, a Javanese-like five-note scale). Melodies are written as scale degrees, so each idiom keeps to its own notes.


Events use music.hit(): notes come from the chord playing at that moment, on the next 16th note. Mushrooms climb the chord as an arpeggio when collected quickly; gems play two notes; being spotted sounds a sonar ping on the beat; a hit is a low timpani; levels and game over get short phrases.


The characters follow Heider and Simmel's 1944 film, in which people read chases, bullying and protection into a moving circle, two triangles and a box.


const music = await startAdaptive({composition, plan});
music.set({threat: 0.7, health: 0.3, level: 2});      // re-arranged from the next bar
music.hit('chime', {tones: [0, 1, 2], spread: 1});   // root, third, fifth on the next 16ths
