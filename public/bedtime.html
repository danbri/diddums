What the extraction does—and does not—claim

Pitch and attack candidates came from recording-specific harmonic templates and non-negative spectral decomposition, with limited spectral review. The result has not been checked note by note.

Durations are estimated playback gates, not exact key-release measurements. Velocities approximate relative dynamics, not the keyboard's original MIDI values. No pedal movements were recovered. The two parts are split by register, not verified hand assignment.

Rows marked check particularly need review; unmarked rows can also contain mistakes. Notes already sounding at the start are placed at time zero.
